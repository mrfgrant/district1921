About

Mama's Southern Kitchen has been feeding Augusta since 1987. Founded by Dorothy "Mama" Williams, our family recipes have been passed down through four generations — from her grandmother's kitchen in Eatonton, Georgia to our dining room today.

We specialize in authentic soul food prepared daily from scratch: fried chicken, catfish, oxtails, collard greens, cornbread, and our famous Sunday buffet. Catering available for events up to 500 guests.
